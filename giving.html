Giving

Giving is part of our worship.

We give because God has first given to us, and because we want to play our part in His work here on earth.

Why We Give

Giving is an integral part of our relationship with God.

  • It acknowledges Him as the giver of everything we have.
  • It reflects our trust that He will continue to provide for us.
  • It allows us to be part of His work here on earth.
  • It is a way for us to follow through on what He has asked us to do.
  • It changes the world for the glory of God: the gospel is preached, lives are touched, people are fed, bodies are healed, the Church grows, and we, in turn, are blessed by God so that we can give again.

The word "tithe" literally means "a tenth", so, as believers today, we worship the Lord with the tithe, or the first ten percent of our income.

At Kingfisher we do not take a collection during the services, as we do not want this to become a focus of our time together. While we teach the biblical principle of tithing, we strongly believe the amount each person gives is to be a confidential, free-will offering, decided between them and God.

Church Tithes Too

We give from what we are given.

Kingfisher Church also tithes 10% of all the income we receive. The trustees decide who receives our tithe, and we regularly give to local, national and international charities, organisations and individuals.

We would love to hear suggestions for ways our tithe giving can support worthy causes, so please contact us if you have an idea.
